description: "AI capability at any fixed level becomes nearly free within roughly two years of its release due to predictable hardware improvements, model distillation, and vendor competition. This collapsing cost floor makes model access a wasting asset that cannot sustain long-term moats, while total industry spending rises as usage volume explodes. Products relying solely on smarter models will face rapid margin compression as open-weight alternatives close the capability gap every few months. Sustainable strategy requires building defenses around scarce, non-commoditizing assets like proprietary data, distribution networks, workflow integration, and liability capacity rather than betting on temporary AI access advantages."
