[RT by @AndrewCurran_: In 2031, Earth might no longer be the best place to build a datacenter.
From facilities like Starbase Louisiana, most compute could be deployed into outer space instead.

It's hard to overstate just how much that could change AI policy:

(1) AI policy stops being local: no datacenter protests, no county taxes - anything has to go through the federal authorities that govern space and launches. That's bad news for grassroots activism.

(2) Enforcement becomes very hard: if we deploy space compute without hardware-based oversight, we can't stop constellations of AI agents going rogue. We might want to bake in ways to stop them before launching the satellites.

(3) Middle powers are put on a timer: building datacenters is not a stable endgame when space is an option. You can only negotiate for so many concessions if the labs can always leave the planet instead.

(4) Who will be the Taiwan of space? The bottlenecks for space compute at scale can still be contested. Launch pads to sun-synchronous orbit, solar panels and manufacturing equipment. Anti-satellite weapons, perhaps.
