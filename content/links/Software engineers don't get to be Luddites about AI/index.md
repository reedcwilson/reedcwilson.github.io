description: "Software engineers shouldn't play Luddite about AI because their entire profession has been automating other people's jobs — from bookkeepers to travel agents to trading-floor runners — so complaining when the same force hits them is hypocritical. The author reframes the Luddite analogy: engineers are the smiths who built the power looms, not the weavers who smashed them, and those smiths were themselves displaced fifty years later. You can be anti-AI for climate or social-media reasons, but picking this exact moment to oppose automation is \"undignified.\" The author leans toward automation being a net positive and argues engineers have always been on the side of technological progress, whether they admit it or not."
