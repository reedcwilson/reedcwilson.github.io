description: "Rapid advances in AI are prompting mathematicians to confront the possibility of being eclipsed, though most currently view the technology as a complementary tool rather than an immediate replacement. Historically, automation has pushed mathematical work upward toward novel theory-building and problem-framing while reclassifying routine calculations as mere computation. Current AI applications focus on literature navigation, filling proof gaps, and accelerating guided research, but experts warn these capabilities will likely continue scaling beyond human limits. Even if AI eventually surpasses humans in all mathematical tasks, the field's enduring value lies in community, education, and pursuing diverse intellectual goals that extend far beyond solving open problems."
