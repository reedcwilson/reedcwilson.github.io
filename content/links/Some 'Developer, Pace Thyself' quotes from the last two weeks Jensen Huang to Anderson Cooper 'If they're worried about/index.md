title: "Some 'Developer, Pace Thyself' quotes from the last two weeks:

Jensen Huang to Anderson Cooper:
'If they're worried about it, they should just stop. If the product is not safe, just don't release it.'

'First of all, they ought to have self-restraint. If they're telling everybody that their product is unsafe, they obviously know it. They're publicly admitting it. They ought to just not release it.'

Jensen Huang to Ezra Klein:
'If they believe they’re out of control, then the right answer is: Don’t ship products until they’re in control.'

David Sacks here on platform:
'So go ahead and pace the frontier. You are the ones setting it. The easiest way not to build superintelligence is for you to agree not to build it. Demanding your preferred regulatory framework as the price of that will look like blackmail of the public and the political system. So just do it.'

Mark Zuckerberg to NBC News:
'I don’t think that we need some kind of industrywide coordination. I think that each lab needs to take the time, and when it sees that there are issues, you just take the time that you need internally to basically make sure that you’re proceeding safely.'

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent to CNBC:
'The Hugging Face incident, that is the responsibility of the OpenAI management, not a bunch of agents. So what the president was saying is that we cannot say, ‘Oh, we absolve you of responsibility and the government’s going to take responsibility.’ These labs need to take responsibility for themselves. They can slow down any time they want to.'

Michael Kratsios to Fox News Sunday:
'It’s very interesting that they talk about these issues, about how scared they are about potentially where the future is, and maybe they want to do a pause. Our position is, if you do believe that you’re developing a technology that is unsafe or you don’t want it out into the world, you can just stop it. You don’t need someone to force you to do that.'"
