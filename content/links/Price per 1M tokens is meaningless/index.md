description: "Comparing AI models by price per million tokens is misleading because inconsistent tokenizers and wildly different token efficiency make the metric unreliable. Hidden reasoning steps and varying output quality mean cheaper-per-token models often require significantly more tokens to complete actual tasks, negating their savings. Real-world value should be measured by cost per completed task or benchmark result rather than sticker pricing. Selecting models based on per-token rates will likely lead to higher bills and worse performance."
