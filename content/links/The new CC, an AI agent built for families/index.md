description: "Google is expanding CC, its personal daily-brief agent, into a family/household agent with its own verified Google account that up to six members can collaborate with. Each member controls what they share (school emails, vet notes, etc.), and CC auto-generates a shared morning brief, syncs events into a family Calendar/Tasks, and handles chores like filling permission slips, building meal plans, and checking drive times. It runs on Google's Antigravity agentic harness and Gemini models, connecting to Gmail, Chat, Docs, and Calendar. Currently an early Google Labs experiment, US-only, 18+, with existing users getting an upgrade email and new users joining a waitlist."
