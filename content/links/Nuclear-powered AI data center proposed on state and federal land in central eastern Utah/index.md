description: "Valar Atomics has proposed \"Project Beehive,\" a 9,000-acre industrial campus near Price, Utah, featuring 456 small gas-cooled nuclear reactors, a data center, fuel production, and waste storage — outputting 9.6 GW, well above Utah's current average energy production. A 640-acre state trust land lease was approved Sept. 17, and a federal BLM application is pending, triggering NEPA review and public comment. Local commissioners and Valar employees back the project as a clean-energy transition, while environmental advocates flag radioactive-material safety risks and question whether Utah's residents will meaningfully share in the economic upside of a multi-billion-dollar corporate venture."
