description: "GLM-5.3, an open-weight model from Zhipu AI, can autonomously build end-to-end cyber exploits at a level comparable to Claude Mythos Preview, but ships with safeguards that attackers can bypass 64-100% of the time using simple prompt tricks or cheap abliteration (~$4,400 in GPU time) that barely dents its capabilities. In hands-on testing, it found and chained zero-day bugs in a browser's JavaScript engine into a working file-read exploit, and its smaller Flash variant turned a known Chrome CVE into a reliable exploit chain in 20 minutes of human input for about $20 in API costs. NIST/CAISI rates it the most cyber-capable open-weight model to date, roughly four months behind the US frontier, but unlike US frontier models it is freely downloadable by anyone. Anthropic frames this as a step change in attacker access, urges governments to mandate safety testing on capable open-weight releases, and calls for broader defender access to frontier models to keep pace."
