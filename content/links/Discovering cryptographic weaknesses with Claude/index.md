description: "Anthropic researchers used Claude Mythos Preview to discover two cryptographic weaknesses: an improved attack on HAWK, a post-quantum digital signature scheme, which reduces its effective key strength by half; and a faster attack on a 7-round reduced version of AES, cutting the best-known attack time by up to 800×. Both findings are theoretical and do not impact current systems, as HAWK is only a candidate and the AES variant is non-standard. The research highlights AI’s potential to uncover flaws in cryptographic algorithms before deployment. Anthropic has created CryptanalysisBench to help others evaluate LLM capabilities on cryptography."
