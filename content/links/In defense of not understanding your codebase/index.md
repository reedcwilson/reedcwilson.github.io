description: "In large, complex codebases with high turnover, complete understanding is impossible and unnecessary; engineers must instead work effectively with partial knowledge. Modern systems are too vast to rewrite from scratch or fully reverse-engineer after staff changes, so developers should focus on local reasoning, make educated guesses, and iterate incrementally. Maintaining a perfect mental model of the code is just one engineering value among many, routinely traded off for speed, compliance, security patches, and external dependencies. Accepting partial understanding as normal allows teams to ship work reliably without waiting for mythical complete expertise or blocking progress on theoretical purity."
