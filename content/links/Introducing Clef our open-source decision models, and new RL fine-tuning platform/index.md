description: "Cloudflare is open-sourcing two decision models, Clef and Clef-flash (Apache 2.0, on Hugging Face and Workers AI), that produce typed, probability-scored classifications for agentic workflows — faster and more deterministic than LLMs, and fully Jev-API compatible. Clef uses a Qwen backbone with a non-autoregressive scoring step, a 64k context window, and a vision encoder, beating Jev on latency and matching or exceeding it on most accuracy benchmarks. The company is also launching an RL fine-tuning platform (RLCD) so customers can adapt Clef to domain-specific classification tasks like threat intel, support triage, or bot detection."
