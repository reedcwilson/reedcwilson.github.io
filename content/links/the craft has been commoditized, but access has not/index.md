description: "AI has commoditized the act of writing code, but corporates still gatekeep who is *allowed* to write it, treating software development as a named role rather than a capability everyone can exercise. The author argues the scarce resource is no longer human authorship (which Agile was built around) but organizational access, and that leaders who don't put \"enable non-engineers to ship software\" on their roadmap are self-sabotaging. The reframe for engineers: your job is now to build the systems, safety controls, and guardrails that let everyone in the org ship to production safely, not to be the sole human hand on the keyboard. Quality won't suffer, because current LLMs outperform the code most companies could hire for anyway."
