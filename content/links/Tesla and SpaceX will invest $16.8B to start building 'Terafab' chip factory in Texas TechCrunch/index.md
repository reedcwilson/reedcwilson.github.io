description: "Tesla and SpaceX are co-investing $16.8 billion in Terafab, a massive semiconductor factory in Grimes County, Texas, designed to meet the surging compute demands of AI and robotics. The 100 million square foot facility will employ at least 3,000 workers and produce specialized chips for Tesla’s Optimus robots, Cybercabs, and SpaceX’s space-based data centers. Intel is also contributing to the project, which sources water from a local reservoir rather than groundwater. While officials highlight economic and educational benefits, residents have raised concerns about large tax incentives and transparency surrounding the development."
