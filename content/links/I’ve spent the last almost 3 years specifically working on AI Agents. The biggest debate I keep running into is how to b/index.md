The biggest debate I keep running into is how to build agents that have little to no hallucinations and have almost deterministic workflows.

My belief is this is wasted effort, right now we need to focus on providing broad capabilities to agents. Give the agent access to a library of information. And let it do its thing.

Give your agent the ability to do broad things and then let it check its work.

[I’ve spent the last almost 3 years specifically working on AI Agents.

The biggest debate I keep running into is how to build agents that have little to no hallucinations and have almost deterministic workflows.

My belief is this is wasted effort, right now we need to focus on providing broad capabilities to agents. Give the agent access to a library of information. And let it do its thing.

Give your agent the ability to do broad things and then let it check its work.

I know more than a dozen people that strongly disagree with my analysis here. I’m curious for any of you that build agents not meant for coding how are you thinking about it?](https://nitter.reedcwilson.com/GosuCoder/status/2116520100048072964#m)
